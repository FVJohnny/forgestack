# The App

`app/dashboard` is the authenticated area, guarded client-side by
`ProtectedRoute` and server-validated by the backend's `JwtAuthGuard`. It is a
deliberately simple user-facing app for you to expand into your own product.
Operational statistics are not shown here: metrics, logs, and traces live in
Grafana (see [Monitoring](/docs/monitoring)).

The app is composed of pages under `app/dashboard/`:

- **Home** (`page.tsx`): a welcome header, the message-of-the-day banner, quick
  links, an account summary, and the user's recent sign-in activity (last
  login, known IPs, and devices) read from the JWT-protected
  `/auth/me/activity` endpoint.
- **Tasks** (`tasks/page.tsx`): a personal todo list, the worked example of
  calling a second backend service (see
  [Calling Backend Services](/docs/frontend/backend-services)). Create,
  complete, and delete tasks; each request carries the auth cookie and the
  backend enforces ownership.
- **Account** (`account/page.tsx`): profile details and password change.
- **Admin** (`admin/page.tsx`, admins only): user management (list, filter,
  suspend, delete) and the message-of-the-day editor.

Shared chrome lives in `app/dashboard/components/` (`sidebar`, `topbar`,
`page-header`, `motd-banner`, `account-mini-card`).
